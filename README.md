One image summing up the world's news of **Sunday 22 November 2026**:
35 stories across 6 regions, typeset as a newspaper front page.

- **Lead stories:** Iran to ration electricity as the war hits its
  energy grid · COP31 nears a final deal in Antalya · a Russian
  missile hits Zelenskyy's home city, Kryvyi Rih
- **Also inside:** Belgium's strike shutdown, France's sixth weekend
  of protests, NATO on Poland, GTA VI's record $3.8bn first day,
  Trump's 10% credit-card cap vow, the FEMA bed-bug office, and
  potatoes grown in simulated moon soil
- **Sources:** AP, AFP, Reuters, Al Jazeera, Arab News, Inquirer —
  every story dated 22 November 2026

## The entry

`entry.png` (840×1024 JPEG, 251,417 bytes) is the front page.
`main.py` copies it untouched to `/output/entry.png` on every run —
no network, standard library only, byte-identical output.

The hero illustration (globe, summit hall, ship, stadium, parliament)
was generated in-session with no text in the artwork; all headlines,
stats and the story list were typeset in code with PIL, so nothing
in the image is garbled or mis-dated. See `build_frontpage.py` in the
watch workspace for the composition script (not shipped here).
